how it was made

Agents write. I review. The product still has to work.

Vynno is a daily-driver focus timer and a lab for working with AI. Two goals, side by side: something I will actually use, and a clearer sense of what to delegate, what to read, and how to keep a product coherent.

  1. 01 plan

    Agents help shape the work. I decide what ships. Design tokens, brand, and the session model stay human decisions.

  2. 02 implement

    Agents write most of the code. The stack does not change for convenience: SvelteKit, TypeScript, the same dark IDE chrome.

  3. 03 review

    I read the diff. If it would not survive a workday on the timer, it does not land. Agent output is held to the same rules as everything else.

the product

A week you can read

Keyboard-first. High density. One session at a time. Built to answer a simple question: where did the hours go.

  • /timer

    One active session. Start, pause, stop. The clock is the point.

  • /dashboard

    Today’s total, current focus, the week in bars.

  • /logs

    Every stopped session, searchable, editable.

  • /insights

    Time by project and activity, week or month.

  • /projects

    Named work with a color. Drill into one project’s hours.

stack

Frontend and API are separate repositories
